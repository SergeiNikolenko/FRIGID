MARLIN · recorded decode · every attempt of one spectrum

Eight attempts, one spectrum: where they split, and where they died.

Not the gold answer walked forwards — the run itself. Each attempt starts from the same mass and its own dropout of the fingerprint, writes eight positions per forward pass, and ends either as a returned candidate or in a dead end the mask could not get out of. Rows sharing a prefix share a branch; the branch splits at the first position where they disagree.

  • × the token where it left the gold answer

Branch thickness is how many attempts still agree at that point. A split is a position where the sampler, given a different dropout of the same fingerprint, chose a different token.

There is no way back up a branch: the decoder never backtracks. Eight positions are committed in one forward pass, and when the mask leaves nothing to write, the attempt is abandoned and a fresh one starts from the beginning. Every leaf is a whole life of the decoder, not a branch it could retry.
